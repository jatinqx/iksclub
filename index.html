IKS Centre · IISER Pune

The IKS Centre at IISER Pune

The IKS Centre brings Indian Knowledge Systems into IISER Pune’s academic life, encouraging teaching, research, and outreach where classical Indian thought meets modern science. The Pratyabhijñā IKS Club is the student-led community that works alongside the Centre, running discussions, workshops, lectures, and conferences.

About IISER Pune

A Premier Institute for Science Education and Research

IISER Pune is one of India’s leading autonomous institutes dedicated to high-quality science education and frontier research, with a strong national and global academic reputation across disciplines.

Indian Knowledge Systems

A Living Tree of Knowledge

Indian Knowledge Systems form a continuous tradition of inquiry, from foundational mathematics and astronomy to medicine, architecture, linguistics, philosophy, and epistemology. Our club is an open, interdisciplinary space to critically explore this heritage and its historical intersections with science.

About the Club

An Interdisciplinary Community at IISER Pune

What is the Indian Knowledge System?

Bharat has long been known for its civilizational and cultural heritage, and for scientific and technological knowledge that shaped India and contributed to the growth of other parts of the world. The Indian Knowledge System (IKS) is the broad term for this inheritance, which thrived over millennia and is documented in numerous treatises:

Vedic sciencesSpiritualityMathematicsAstronomyChemistry & metallurgyĀyurvedaCivil engineeringArchitectureShip buildingArtha-śāstraYogaArtClassical music

Why it matters today

Traditional knowledge takes a holistic approach, while modern science takes a reductionistic one. Together they offer a strong platform for a resilient future. Reconnecting with IKS in the modern context is essential for a sustainable and equitable world with a moral order.

Club Themes

Exploring the Depths of Ancient Indian Scientific Knowledge

🔭

Astronomy and Mathematics

Exploring ancient Indian contributions to astronomy, astronomical observations, mathematical principles, and their applications in understanding the cosmos and developing computational methods.

⚗️

Chemistry, Metallurgy and Material Science

Metals in medicines and materials: investigating ancient Indian metallurgical practices, the use of metals in traditional medicine, and advanced material science from ancient texts.

🌿

Āyurveda and Personal Medicine

Understanding ancient Indian medical wisdom, personalized medicine approaches, herbal remedies, and their relevance to modern healthcare and wellness.

🏛️

Architectural Wonder

Ancient temple building technology: examining the engineering marvels, architectural principles, and construction techniques that created enduring monuments.

📜

Scientific Language and Grammar

Evolution of scientific Indian language and grammar: studying how ancient languages facilitated the documentation and transmission of scientific knowledge.

Our Vision

To provide a platform for interdisciplinary scientific dialogue that bridges ancient wisdom with modern science, bringing together scholars and enthusiastic students from different disciplines to encourage exploration, research, and collaboration. In line with the National Education Policy (2020), we emphasize revisiting and integrating ancient Indian knowledge, and understanding it from a modern scientific perspective.

Objectives

  • Explore India’s scientific and technological heritage through rigorous interdisciplinary dialogue
  • Bridge ancient Indian knowledge with contemporary science, education, and innovation
  • Promote sustainable, evidence-informed approaches rooted in Indian knowledge traditions

Outcomes

  • Research exposure to IKS: Science and Technology through expert-led sessions
  • Identification of research gaps, priorities, and future directions in IKS
  • Stronger collaborative networks and consortium planning for IKS activities
  • Better course-level integration and engagement among young researchers in IKS studies

Pratyabhijñā — Our Name

Etymology, Philosophy, and Identity

📖

Etymology

प्रति (prati) + अभि (abhi) + ज्ञा (jñā) — प्रति means 'again, back, toward'; अभि means 'toward, directly, distinctly'; ज्ञा means 'to know, to recognize'. Together: 'to recognize again what was already there.' In the context of IKS, this reflects the idea of returning to classical texts and recognizing the ideas, knowledge, and insights within them.

🔱

Philosophical Meaning

In Kashmir Śaivism, Pratyabhijñā refers to the recognition of one's true nature. The truth is not something newly acquired; it is already present and becomes evident through recognition. The limited 'I' → Recognition → The Universal 'I'. This is the philosophical core that guides our club's inquiry into Indian Knowledge Systems.
